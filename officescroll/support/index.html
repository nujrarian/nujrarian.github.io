Office Scroll Support

Need help with the game, or want to tell us about a problem? Write to officescroll.support@gmail.com. It helps if you include your device model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

I changed phones. How do I get my progress back?
On your old phone, open Settings in the game and make sure Cloud Backup is on. It uses your Game Center account. On the new phone, sign in to the same Game Center account and open the game: your progress is restored by itself.

How do I stop the game sending play statistics?
Open Settings in the game and turn Play Data off.

How do I have my data deleted?
Write to the address above and we will delete your online save and any statistics linked to it.
