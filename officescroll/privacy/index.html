Office Scroll Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Office Scroll is a mobile game made by Arjun Nair ("we"). This page explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, write to officescroll.support@gmail.com.

The short version

  • You can play without creating an account or giving us your name, email address or phone number. We never ask for them.
  • Your game progress is saved on your device and backed up online so you do not lose it.
  • Anonymous play statistics are sent only if you say yes when the game asks. You can change your answer at any time in Settings.
  • Buying Coins is optional. Purchases are handled by Apple, and we never see your payment details.
  • Ads appear only when you choose to watch a video for a reward. They are not personalised, and the game does not ask to track you across other apps.
  • We do not sell your information.

What the game stores and sends

Your game progress

Your progress (level, XP, Coins, the items you own and have equipped, best scores, challenge progress and daily streak) is stored on your device. A copy is kept online with Unity Cloud Save, a service run by Unity Technologies, so that it can be restored if you reinstall the game or change phones.

To keep that copy, the game signs in to Unity's services with a random player ID created for your installation. The ID is not your name and is not linked to any contact details.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, or you turn on Cloud Backup in Settings, the game links your player ID to your Game Center account. This is what lets your progress follow you to another device. We receive only the identifier Game Center gives this game for you. We do not receive your Apple ID, name, email address, friends list or contacts.

Play statistics (only with your permission)

The first time you open the game it asks whether it may send anonymous play statistics. If you say yes, the game sends information about how it is played through Unity Analytics. This includes:

  • when a play session starts and ends
  • which level a shift was played in, how long it lasted, the score, and how it ended
  • which in-game items were bought with Coins
  • how far a new player gets through the first shift
  • general device information such as device model, operating system version, language and country

If you say no, none of this is sent. You can turn it on or off at any time under Settings, Play Data.

Purchases

You can buy packs of Coins if you want to. Purchases are made through the App Store and are processed by Apple. We do not receive your name, card number or any other payment details. The game receives a receipt from the App Store confirming the purchase. It uses the receipt to add the Coins and to make sure each purchase is paid out only once, and it keeps a record of recent receipts and the number of Coins bought with your game progress.

If you have allowed play statistics, they include the fact that a purchase was made, which pack it was and its price.

Advertising

The game shows a video ad only when you choose to watch one in return for a reward, such as extra Coins. No ads are shown at any other time.

Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay by the Unity Ads and ironSource Ads networks, which are run by Unity Technologies. To deliver an ad, count whether it was shown and guard against fraud, these services receive information from your device when the game asks for an ad. This includes:

  • your IP address, from which a general location such as your country can be worked out
  • device model, operating system version, language and screen size
  • an identifier for this installation of the game on your device
  • which ads were shown and whether they were watched or tapped

The game does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so your device's advertising identifier is not shared, and the game tells the ad services not to personalise ads. Ads may still be chosen from context, such as the game being played, your country and your type of device.

If you have allowed play statistics, they include the fact that a video was watched and which reward it was for.

What we do not collect

The game does not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, photos, contacts or microphone or camera input. If you share a results card from the game, the picture is handed to your phone's own share sheet; the game does not send it anywhere itself.

Who else is involved

The game uses Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save and Analytics), provided by Unity Technologies. Unity processes the information described above on our behalf. Unity's own privacy policy is at https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Ads are provided through Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads and ironSource Ads, also run by Unity Technologies, which handle the advertising information described above under the same policy.

On Apple devices, Game Center and App Store purchases are provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, at https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

How long information is kept

Your online save is kept for as long as you keep playing, so that it can be restored. Play statistics are kept in a form that does not identify you and are used to understand and improve the game. Purchase records are kept by Apple under its own policy. Information handled by the ad services is kept by them for as long as their policy describes.

Your choices

  • Turn play statistics on or off under Settings, Play Data.
  • Choose not to watch reward videos: the game never shows an ad you did not ask for.
  • Ask us to delete your online save and any statistics linked to your player ID by writing to officescroll.support@gmail.com. Deleting the game from your device removes the copy stored on the device.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have further rights over your information, such as the right to see it or have it corrected. Write to us and we will help.

Children

Office Scroll is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
